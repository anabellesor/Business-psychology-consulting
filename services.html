Our Services

At Business Psychology Consulting, we provide evidence-based organizational solutions designed to help businesses grow, optimize performance, and develop high-impact leaders. Our services integrate Industrial and Organizational Psychology principles with practical business strategy to support measurable results.

Training & development

Develop engaging training content, including PowerPoint presentations, e-learning modules, and video-based learning experiences.

Job analysis

Systematic analyses of job roles to identify essential duties, required skills, and organizational fit, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives.

Performance evaluation

360 feedback systems utilized to capture evaluations from supervisors, peers, subordinates, and customers to foster holistic employee growth.

Marketing

General guidance and the creation of images and video to building professional online platforms, curating promotional content, and managing brand identity.

Psychometric Assessments

Psychometrics tools developed and validated to improve hiring decisions, reduce turnover, and identify high-potential talent.

Competency Evaluations

Knowledge and skill competency tests to ensure candidates meet minimum job requirements and organizational expectations.

Predictive Analytics

Statistical models that can be utilized to forecast outcomes such as turnover, productivity, and sales growth, allowing for proactive decision-making.

Survey & Instrument Development

Design, validate, and administer surveys and assessments (e.g., employee engagement, cultural climate, job satisfaction, turnover intentions) tailored to organizational needs.
