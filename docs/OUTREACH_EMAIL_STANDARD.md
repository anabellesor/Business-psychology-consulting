# Sales email standard

Every outbound sales draft must follow these rules:

1. Use a concise, benefit-focused subject that does not repeat the prospect's company name or Business Psychology Consulting.
2. Personalize the opening and proposed service to the prospect's observable needs.
3. Keep Darren Irwin as the Business Development sender and relationship owner.
4. Use the approved Anabelle escalation paragraph only when research-intensive or strategic needs are relevant.
5. End with Darren's compact, mobile-readable signature.
6. Place a visible Unsubscribe button after the signature using:
   mailto:businesspsychologyllc@gmail.com?subject=Unsubscribe
7. Include this plain-language footer:
   “This is a one-to-one business outreach message. If it is not relevant or you prefer not to receive follow-up messages, use the unsubscribe button or reply ‘unsubscribe.’”
8. Include the company identity and a valid physical postal address in every commercial email before sending.
9. Record every opt-out immediately in the master CRM and prevent all automated follow-up for that contact and organization as appropriate.
10. Include © 2026 Business Psychology Consulting. All rights reserved.

Important: “Daytona Beach, Florida 32114” is currently public on the website but may not satisfy commercial-email postal-address requirements by itself. Add the company’s valid street address, registered commercial mailbox, or qualifying post-office box before scaled outreach.

The unsubscribe action must remain visible in both HTML and plain-text versions. Do not use deceptive subjects, hidden text, remote tracking pixels, or fabricated personalization.
